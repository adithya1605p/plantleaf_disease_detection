Edge AI Architecture & Pipeline

Real-time inspection of the four-phase computer vision and expert agronomic system pipeline running on your mobile or desktop browser.

STAGE 01

Foliage Feature Extraction

YOLOv8 deep convolutional backbone processes high-resolution leaf imagery, identifying multi-scale pathology features and lesion focal boundaries.

STAGE 02

Grad-CAM Explainability

Calculates class-activation maps and visual saliency gradients to highlight exactly which foliar regions triggered the diagnostic decision.

STAGE 03

Severity Surface Integration

Computes bounding box union and canopy area ratios to classify lesion proliferation as Mild, Moderate, or Severe.

STAGE 04

Agronomic Prescription

Resolves matched pathogens against the curated phytosanitary database, generating organic, chemical, and cultural remediation protocols.
